Detailed Description

Loudness meter per ITU-R BS.1770-4 / EBU R128.

Call process() from the audio thread. The momentary (400 ms) and short-term (3 s) values are updated every 100 ms and can be read from any thread. Integrated loudness and loudness range are gated measurements over the whole history since the last reset; they are computed on demand from that history by getIntegrated() / getLoudnessRange(), which are intended to be called from the message thread as they do a little work.

Channel weighting follows the standard: 1.0 for the first three channels (L, R, C) and 1.41 for channels 4 and 5 (Ls, Rs).

Values are in LUFS (LU for the range). Below the measurable floor, or before any audio has been processed, getters return silence.
